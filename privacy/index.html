Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Sun-Cy Shipyard is a one-person game studio in Quebec. This page explains what personal information the studio collects, through this website and its games' playtest builds, why, who else handles it, and how you can see, correct or delete yours.

It follows Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as updated by Law 25.

Person in charge

The person in charge of the protection of personal information is the studio's founder and owner. Write to this address for any question, request or complaint about your personal information:

Founder and owner, person in charge of the protection of personal information

Sun-Cy Shipyard, Quebec, Canada

hello@suncyshipyard.com

What we collect, and why

  • The contact form: your name, your email address, the topic you pick and your message. We use them only to read your message and reply to you.
  • Emails you send us: your email address, your name and what you write, to reply and keep track of the conversation.
  • Playtest builds: test versions of our games keep a log of your play on your computer: what you click, buy and build, errors, your computer's basic specs, and any notes and screenshots you leave. Nothing is sent automatically. We receive the log only if you choose to send us the file, and we use it only to fix and improve the game.
  • Technical information: when you visit the site, your browser sends your IP address and browser details to the services that deliver it (GitHub for the pages, Google Fonts for the fonts). The studio doesn't receive or use this information.

We don't add you to a mailing list, use your information for marketing, or sell or rent it to anyone. The site uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing on it identifies, locates or profiles you.

Who else handles your information

These service providers handle personal information for the studio, only to provide their service:

  • Web3Forms (Web3Creative, India; servers on Amazon Web Services): delivers contact form messages to our email. It keeps a copy of each message for up to 3 years, then deletes it automatically, and may check your IP address and email address with spam filters (CleanTalk and Akismet).
  • Titan (Titan Solution Ltd, Cayman Islands): hosts our email, where your messages and our replies are stored.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States): hosts this website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, United States): delivers the fonts. It receives your IP address, browser details and the address of the page, sets no cookies, and Google says it doesn't use this information for profiles or advertising.

We share personal information with no one else, unless the law requires it.

Information sent outside Quebec

These providers store or process information outside Quebec, in the United States, India and other countries. Before using them, we assessed how they protect personal information (a privacy impact assessment), and we use them under their terms of service and data-processing agreements.

How long we keep it

  • Messages (contact form and email): up to 2 years after our last exchange, then deleted. Conversations with press and content creators may be kept while we're working together.
  • Web3Forms' copy of form messages: deleted by Web3Forms after at most 3 years.
  • Playtest logs you send us: until the fixes are done, and at most 6 months after the game's release, then deleted.
  • Technical logs kept by GitHub and Google: under their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time, free of charge, to:

  • tell you whether we hold personal information about you, and give you a copy (also in a common, structured format if you ask);
  • correct information that is wrong, incomplete or unclear;
  • delete your information, or stop using it, and withdraw your consent;
  • stop disseminating your information, or de-index any link to it.

Write to hello@suncyshipyard.com. We'll reply in writing within 30 days. To protect your information, we may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by writing from the email address we know.

Complaints: write to us first, and we'll answer within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Children

This site isn't made for children. If you're under 14, ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Security

Only the studio's owner has access to the messages and playtest logs. The email and domain accounts are protected by unique passwords and two-factor authentication, and the whole site is served over HTTPS.

If something goes wrong

If personal information is lost, or accessed or shared without authorization (a confidentiality incident), we act right away to reduce the harm, record the incident in our register, and, if there's a risk of serious injury, notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and the people affected.

How we manage personal information

The studio's rules for handling personal information, approved by the person in charge on November 7, 2026:

  • Roles: the owner is the only person who handles personal information, from collection to deletion, and is responsible for this policy.
  • Keeping and deleting: information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above (see "How long we keep it"), then deleted.
  • Requests and complaints: sent to hello@suncyshipyard.com, handled by the person in charge, answered in writing within 30 days.
  • New tools: before adopting a new service that handles personal information, or sending information outside Quebec, we assess its effect on privacy and update this page.
  • Incidents: every confidentiality incident is recorded in a register, as described above.
  • Review: this policy is reviewed every year and whenever the site changes how it handles personal information.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
